Frequently Asked

Questions

Find answers to common questions about International Ecademy, courses, registration and online learning.

Students can register online for any course at any time and progress through the course at their own pace. All of the course content is available online for students to work through.

Assignments are submitted to the online dropbox within the learning environment. Tests and quizzes are also completed online and are open-book and timed.

The final exam is completed online under the supervision of an appropriate proctor. Students choose the time, date and location of the final exam, but must have an approved proctor present to supervise the exam.

When the course is finished, International Ecademy will send official copies of the final report card to the student's home address and, where applicable, to the student's home school for inclusion on the Ontario Student Transcript (OST).

Each course contains instructional content covering all Ontario curriculum expectations. Assignments and tests are placed throughout the course for students to complete and submit online.

Interactive activities and multimedia elements are included throughout the courses to engage students, reinforce understanding, provide practice opportunities, and differentiate instruction.

Students may opt to work at an accelerated, traditional, or extended pace. However, regular work submission and communication are required in all International Ecademy courses in accordance with Ontario Ministry of Education policies.

International Ecademy has ongoing registration. Students may start a course at any time throughout the year.

To graduate with an Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD), students must earn the required credits, participate in community involvement activities, and complete the provincial secondary school literacy requirement.

Students must earn the required compulsory credits to obtain the Ontario Secondary School Diploma.

Students must complete a minimum of 40 hours of community involvement activities. These activities may be completed at any time while students earn their secondary school credits.

All students must successfully complete the Ontario Secondary School Literacy Test (OSSLT) in order to earn a secondary school diploma.

The test is based on Ontario curriculum expectations for language and communication, particularly reading and writing, up to and including Grade 9.

The OSSLT is the standard method for assessing the literacy skills of students in Ontario for the purpose of determining whether they meet the provincial secondary school literacy requirement for graduation.

Students who write the OSSLT but do not succeed must retake the test and complete it successfully in order to qualify for a secondary school diploma. There is no limit to the number of times the test may be retaken.

The Ontario Secondary School Literacy Course (OSSLC) provides students who have been unsuccessful on the OSSLT with intensive support in achieving the required reading and writing competencies, as well as an alternative means of demonstrating their literacy skills.

Students who have had two opportunities to take the OSSLT and have failed it at least once are eligible to enroll in the OSSLC.

Any high school or adult student may apply to International Ecademy.

International Ecademy accepts international high school and adult students.

Part-time students may enroll for academic enrichment, accelerate their progress in a traditional high school program, or complete unfinished coursework.

Students who intend to graduate from International Ecademy may apply to pursue full-time studies.

Students can upload and share materials, participate in online discussions and chats, and review assignments.

This experience prepares students to adapt to future academic or career environments in an increasingly digital world.

International Ecademy is available 24/7, offering flexible scheduling to complete coursework and providing one-to-one interaction between students and instructors.

As such, it is a viable option for older high school students who wish to earn a high school diploma without returning to a traditional classroom environment.

International Ecademy instructors guide students through the online coursework, and students can complete the work at their own pace.
