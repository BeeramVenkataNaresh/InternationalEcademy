Caliber Intelligence Quotient Test (CIQT)

Brain Checker™ has put in over several years in designing and developing the Caliber Intelligence Quotient Test also known as CIQT. The Brain Checker Caliber Intelligence Quotient Test has been co-developed by Dr. Jonathan Oliver, PhD Psychology, USA , Dr. Jeffery Smith, PhD Psychology, UK and Brain Checker India. The Test has received validations from noted psychologists Dr. Peter Blair, Dr. Greg Wallace, having combined experience of over 50 years in Psychology. The CIQ Testing Program was specifically created for Indian Students keeping in mind our dynamic cultural differences, level of education & academic development of children which is largely different from western countries. Tests that have large scale acceptance in western countries are designed keeping the western education system in context, which then loses it's relevance in India due to our own unique education system.

CIQT is an India’s First Indianised proprietary test developed to understand the Intelligence Quotient of an Indian student. CIQT scores provide the parents with in-depth understanding of their child’s current academic caliber which in turn helps in sorting of compatible career choices. CIQT is a 60 minute test for students aged 13 years and above and is currently administered in India in 13 different languages. The purpose of CIQT Testing, Evaluation and Report in multiple languages is to make this program affordable and accessible to students in urban and rural markets in equal measure.

CIQT Evaluates a Child on the following parameters:

 
Retention
Numerical Handling
Sensing
Word Understanding
Spatial Observations
 
 
Spatial Patterns
Logical Analysis
Arithmetic Chronology
Spatial Co-Relations
Creative Application
