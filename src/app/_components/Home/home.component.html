Why International Ecademy

  • International Ecademy is offering an exciting opportunity for students with high IQs to earn a Canadian diploma certification from their home country.
  • This program could be an excellent stepping stone for gaining admission to top universities, which may lead to transformative opportunities in their future careers.
  • By obtaining this Canadian certification, students may enhance their academic credentials and gain a competitive edge when applying for higher education programs, both locally and internationally.
  • International Ecademy offers Canadian Diploma Certificate (OSSD) from the Ontario Ministry of Education which is valid to get admission world wide.
