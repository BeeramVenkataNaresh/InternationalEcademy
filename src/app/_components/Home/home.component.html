Why International Ecademy

  • International Ecademy offers an exciting opportunity for students to earn a Canadian curriculum certification — the Ontario Secondary School Diploma — while studying from their home country.
  • This program serves as a strong foundation for students seeking admission to top universities, opening doors to greater academic and career opportunities.
  • By obtaining this Canadian certification, students can strengthen their academic credentials and gain a competitive advantage when applying to higher education institutions locally and internationally.
  • The Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD) offered through International Ecademy is recognized globally and supports university admissions worldwide.
